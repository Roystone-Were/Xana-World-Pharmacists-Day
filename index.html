Why we are walking

One walk, two messages: our neighbourhood pharmacies and our Hub.

Standalone pharmacies

Meet the teams at TRM Drive, Lumumba Drive and Githurai 44, the branches that serve walkers every day along this very route.

XanaPlus Hub, Ruiru

Finish at the Hub and see what XanaPlus brings together: wider stock, clinical services and one connected care point for the community.
